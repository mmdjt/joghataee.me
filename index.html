About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Software Engineering at Auburn University, advised by Dr. Ashish Gupta and Dr. Xiao Qin. I also completed an M.S. in Management of Information Systems at the Harbert College of Business. My work sits between messy hospital text, foundation models, and prototypes a clinician can actually inspect.

Medical NLP

Notes, not just tables

Fine-tune open-source models to summarize unstructured clinical notes, extract themes, strip PII, and keep pipelines HIPAA- and IRB-safe.

EHR models

Rare infections

Validated models on 11,000+ multi-site encounters for unusual fungal infections and tuberculosis. Report calibration, sensitivity limits, and site-shift risk.

Neuro + wearables

PTSD and movement

Interpretable deep models on resting-state fMRI for PTSD vs. post-concussive syndrome, plus wearable activity work with Mayo Clinic collaborators.

Research

Current threads. Methods have to be reproducible, and claims have to match what retrospective data can support.

2022 – present

Clinical ML & generative AI

Research assistant at Auburn with Mayo Clinic collaboration. HIPAA-compliant note summarization, rare-infection EHR models, SHAP/ablation with clinician review, and ICU time-series / activity pipelines.

Foundation models

Inspectable clinical text

Prompt design and fine-tuning of open-source LLMs for rare-disease notes. Automated PII removal so note models can run under institutional constraints.

Earlier

Biomedical NLP

At Iran University of Science and Technology: biomedical relation extraction and knowledge-graph construction from scientific text, plus cleaning and deployment pipelines.
