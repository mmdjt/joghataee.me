Work

CS Ph.D. at Auburn with Ashish Gupta and Xiao Qin. I train models on hospital notes and EHR data, then check whether a clinician can actually use the output.

01 — Notes

Clinical NLP

Fine-tune open-source models to summarize unstructured notes and pull themes from rare-disease records. PII stripping so the pipeline can run under HIPAA / IRB rules.

02 — EHR

Rare infections

Models on 11,000+ multi-site encounters for unusual fungal infections and tuberculosis. Calibration and site-shift reported; not framed as a screening product.

03 — Brain + movement

PTSD fMRI and wearables

Interpretable models on resting-state fMRI for PTSD vs. post-concussive syndrome. Separate thread with Mayo on ICU time-series and accelerometer activity labels.
